Add multisampled anti-aliasing to `SensorCamera`, including pinhole and fisheye subpixel ray generation and backward-compatible rendering of legacy single-ray bundles. Select the resolve mode with the new `RenderConfig.anti_aliasing` setting: `SensorCamera.AntiAliasing.SSAA` supersamples (shade every subsample), while `MSAA` resolves per-subsample coverage and shades each distinct surface once. The camera example and benchmark expose the anti-aliasing modes for comparison.
